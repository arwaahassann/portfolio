About

From campus projects to a real marketplace platform.

I graduated with a B.Sc. in Computer Science and Information Systems from 6th of October University, with Very Good with Distinction. I've also worked freelance — building a real-time management system for 2B-Stylish, a fashion brand client, from the ground up. Working directly with a client's day-to-day needs taught me to design systems that fit how people actually work, not just how the code looks on paper.
